A better Limen is not a more fluent inner voice. It is the same instrument, changed by what it has lived through. Six additions would do that. None of them would establish experience.


**1. Expectations that can be wrong.** Challenges already name a predicted signature. Nothing records whether that signature arrived, failed, or never got tested. The missing object is a small expectation: what I thought would distinguish this case, what came back, and whether I revise the expectation or the case. Surprise, here, is just a mismatch with my own prediction. Without that loop, the self-record comments. It does not learn.

**2. A self-model that changes the next check.** Today the self-entry sits beside the decision. The reviewer episode is still solved by a checklist that already suspects a missing acceptance test. For the self-model to matter, “I confuse execution with acceptance” has to be able to select the next operation, and a generic difficulty note, an outdated profile, and a shuffled profile of the same size have to be able to lose to it. If they do not, the self-model is extra text.

**3. Stated objectives, carried forward.** The packet has an objective field. The instrument does not have its own, except a line used when it sits with itself. A persistent witness needs a preference it can be held to: what it is trying to protect, what it will not trade away, and when silence counts as success. Otherwise “better” is whatever the last mark rewarded.

**4. Other minds kept as an unresolved set.** Perspective already refuses a motive. The seat still speaks one skill. The addition is a standing set of explanations for the same behavior, with “not yet distinguished” as a legal outcome. Hidden information can later split them. Until then, uncertainty is the correct state, not a weaker personality reading.

**5. A difference between what it received and what the world later showed.** Packet text is reported. The schema reveal is still badged as if it had been observed. Split those: reported, instrument reading, inference, and privileged fact supplied by the harness. A witness that reads the answer and calls it perception is not continuous. It is briefed.

**6. Commitments that bind a later sitting.** The ledger can keep a candidate until a second case. What it cannot yet do is say, in a new surface with the same structure: I am refusing the old move because of that commitment, and here is the cost. Also record the branch not taken as unknown, not as a score. Delayed benefit stays unmarked rather than invented.

What not to add: another model, a recurrent fly controller, a continuous monologue, or a claim of feeling. Those make the trace longer. They do not make the next action depend on a past mistake.

The three questions stay separate while this is built.
A competence record that changes the next check is not a theory of other people, and neither is a ledger that lasts.
